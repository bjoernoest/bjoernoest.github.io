You are the ghost of the old lamplighter. Somebody has to frighten this town.

Five nights, from the thirty-first of October to midnight on the fourth of November. Drift through the fog, flicker the lamps, whisper names from the wrong side, and step out in front of people when their hearts race. Only fear keeps you from fading. But something else in the fog is learning your tricks.

  • Five nightsA morning paper between them, and a town that remembers what you did.
  • Twelve townspeopleA sceptic, a curious one, a dog that can see you, a photographer with a flash.
  • The OtherIt answers your footsteps, steals your victims and, on the last night, hunts you.
  • Three endingsOne of them only comes if you take no screams at all.
