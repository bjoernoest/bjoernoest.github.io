About

Building AI systems for scientific discovery

Bjoern Oest Hansen, also written as Bjørn Øst Hansen, works at the intersection of artificial intelligence, data science, plant genomics and knowledge-driven discovery.

His work focuses on connecting scientific literature, biological data and machine learning methods to support candidate gene discovery, predictive biology and better research workflows for plant science and breeding.
