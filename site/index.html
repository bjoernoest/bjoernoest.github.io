Why it’s different

Every tycoon has a scarce thing. Here it’s seats.

In RollerCoaster Tycoon you sell rides. In Beer Tent you sell a place on a bench — and there are only so many. Guests turn up in parties of two to ten: thirsty, hungry, bored, needing the loo, running out of patience, and every one of them wants a seat in your tent.

Turnover or merriment?

A table that sings all afternoon is gold for the mood — and blocks ten seats. A table that eats, drinks one Maß and leaves pays the bills but empties the room. Every minute the doors are open, you decide who stays.

What you’ll do

From the first bench to the last call

One day at the fair runs from 11:00 to closing time. Here’s what fills it.

01 · BUILD

Build it, then listen to it

Lay out bench tables, taps, a kitchen, toilets and a band stage on an isometric grid, then dress the place up with flower boxes, hop pillars, lanterns and a Maibaum. When the doors open, click any table and it tells you exactly what it’s waiting for.

  • Benches, taps, kitchen, toilets and a stage for the band
  • Every piece changes how guests walk, queue and feel
  • Ask any table what it needs — it will tell you
02 · RUN

Keep the chaos under control

A tap runs dry. Somebody’s been sick by a table. A stag party is squaring up near the band, and outside the Riesenrad has just broken down. Hire Bedienungen, security, cleaners and Brezn sellers — and decide where every pair of hands goes next.

  • Bedienungen who carry the Maß by the armful
  • Security for the fights, cleaners for… everything else
  • Live alerts and heat maps show where the tent is failing
03 · CELEBRATE

Ride the Stimmung

Stimmung is the tent’s mood, and it’s the meter you get to design. A good band, a well-dressed tent and full benches push it up; empty seats and dry glasses drag it down. Get it high enough and the whole tent rises for a Prosit — and before long somebody is dancing on the benches. (The repairs are on you.)

  • An oompah band with six tunes: polka, waltz, march, Zwiefacher and more
  • Flower boxes, hop pillars, lanterns and a Maibaum
  • Ein Prosit der Gemütlichkeit — the whole tent at once
04 · PLAN

Win the night before

When the doors close, the real decisions start. Read the morning paper and the gossip about your rivals, check tomorrow’s forecast, restock the cellar, and spend the day’s takings on a better band, a bigger kitchen or a marketing push.

  • Festbier, Radler, Weißbier and Alkoholfrei in the cellar
  • A morning paper, a rumour mill and a weather forecast
  • A full office: takings, competition, guests and mood, calendar
05 · TRAVEL

Take the tent on the road

Start at a three-day village Dorffest and work your way up the circuit — Stadtfest, Volksfest, Großes Volksfest — to sixteen days at the Wiesn itself. Every fair has a goal and a prize. Sign with a brewery, rent rides for your pitch, and keep an eye on the sky: rain packs the tents, and a storm shuts every ride.

  • Five fairs, from the village green to the Theresienwiese
  • Brewery deals: cheap volume, premium, or exclusive with a price cap
  • Riesenrad, Wilde Maus, Autoscooter, Kettenflieger and a shooting gallery
06 · COMPETE

Beat the tent across the street

You’re never the only tent. Five rivals want the same crowd, and each has its own idea of a good night: the old-school Alte Linde, the party palace Wolkenschloss, the gourmets at Zum Silbernen Ochsen, the barn-sized Zur Goldenen Gams and the upstart Das Neuling. Take their crowd and they’ll cut their prices to win it back.

  • Six kinds of guest: locals, tourists, stag parties, families, corporate, youths
  • Every guest at the gate picks a tent — make it yours
  • Final standings, profit and prize when the fair is over

Questions

Before you open the doors

What does it cost?

Nothing right now: this is a friends preview. The browser version will stay free when the game goes on sale.

What does it cost?

The store page has the price of the Windows version. The browser version is free and stays free.

What do I need to play?

A 64-bit Windows PC for the download, or any modern desktop browser for the free version. A mouse and keyboard. It’s a 2D game and runs on modest hardware — anything with OpenGL 3.3 graphics, which covers most PCs from the last ten years.

Does it run on a Mac or on Linux?

There’s no download for them yet, but the browser version runs on any desktop. Click “Play free in your browser” and you’re in.

How do I play?

Pick something from the build bar, click to place it, and press Enter to open the doors. With nothing selected, click a table to ask what it’s waiting for. F1 in the game shows every control on one screen.

Where do my saves go?

On your own computer — or, for the browser version, in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Is it finished?

Not quite. Beer Tent is in active development, and this preview is how it gets better: every bug, every balance gripe and every “this bit dragged” helps.
