03 Background

From the bench to building AI functions

I started as a bench scientist studying gene co-expression networks before moving into computational biology, then founding my own knowledge graph startup, OmicsDriven, and eventually setting up and leading KWS's Knowledge Discovery & AI function.

That path, from wet-lab research to data science to organisational leadership, shapes how I work today: I still get hands-on with knowledge graphs, machine learning and generative AI, but spend most of my time turning those capabilities into platforms, workflows and team structures that scientists and breeders actually trust and use.
