A second story in the fog

The Fog Knows

Whatever you photograph stays the way it was.

You arrive in a small town at night and only want to get back to your hotel before midnight. But the town changes whenever you aren't looking: a street closes behind you, the bakery is a playground, the sign spells your street wrong. You have a weak torch, a hand-drawn map, and a Polaroid camera with eight shots. Someone is standing under the lamps. A little closer every time.

  • Eight shotsPhotograph a way through and it stays. Choose carefully.
  • A town that rebuildsWalls across streets, a street that loops, lamps going out behind you.
  • FootstepsThey copy yours. Until you stop, and one more step comes.
  • About seven minutesOne night, one walk, two endings.
